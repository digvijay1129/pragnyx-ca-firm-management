# PragnyX demo brand guidelines

## Brand positioning

**PragnyX** is a fictional accountancy and advisory brand created for the MERN Stack technical assignment. It represents the kind of professional CA-firm website described by the assignment brief; it is not a real firm, registered practice or physical office.

- **Descriptor:** Accountancy & Advisory
- **Tagline:** Clarity in every number. Confidence in every decision.
- **Brand voice:** Clear, measured, helpful, professional and approachable.
- **Visual direction:** Restrained navy, warm brass and cool neutral surfaces; clean sans-serif typography; structured layouts and calm, readable forms.

The `.example` email domain and zero-filled phone are deliberately non-operational. They must never be presented as live contact channels.

## Demo-mode behaviour

`VITE_DEMO_MODE=true` is the assignment default. The UI labels the brand and sample contact values as demo content, avoids clickable `mailto:`/`tel:` actions for the sample values, and explains that inquiry and booking submissions are stored in the assignment database rather than sent to a real CA firm.

The site keeps the demo disclosure visible if demo mode is not explicitly disabled or if the firm email, phone and location are not configured.

## Before a real-world launch

A real business owner must approve the brand descriptor, public contact details, address, office hours, social URLs, website copy and any testimonials. Then replace every sample value and only disable demo mode when the approved information is configured. This assignment sample must not imply that PragnyX is a real regulated professional practice.
